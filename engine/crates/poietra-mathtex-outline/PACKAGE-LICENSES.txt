`poietra-mathtex-outline` package. It is also emitted unchanged as

Aggregate SHA-256: 6a8369948029b4811a906fdd028542d5e34b11044937544a9870a88d4b9cd93a

The aggregate digest is SHA-256 over the ASCII domain
`poietra.mathtex-outline.fonts.v1` followed by one NUL byte, then an unsigned
64-bit big-endian file count, followed for each basename-sorted face by its
unsigned 64-bit big-endian basename length, basename bytes, unsigned 64-bit
big-endian font byte length, and raw font bytes. A Rust unit test recomputes
this value directly from the reachable assets embedded in the runtime.

Noto Sans CJK JP provenance and subset scope
---------------------------------------------

Names: Noto Sans CJK JP Regular and Bold
Version: 2.004
Official distribution: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
Copyright: Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/).
License: SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (reproduced above)

The embedded `NotoSansCJKjp-Regular-Joyo.otf` and
`NotoSansCJKjp-Bold-Joyo.otf` are mechanically reduced copies of face zero in
the corresponding official CJK TTC files. The Regular source SHA-256 is
`b76b0433203017ca80401b2ee0dd69350349871c4b19d504c34dbdd80541690a` and its
embedded subset SHA-256 is
`3b20029c2d55abc5fbaf99a66de9701ed22acd4ab73c85f99d75509e18a08f5c`.
The Bold source SHA-256 is
`faa5f3656a78b2e2d450d27fe8382c778bc2b6bb5ea29c986664a6a435056ceb` and its
embedded subset SHA-256 is
`a8698cb395390eda245e0095537e5459598c16ff236f4d37330b9ef2b66cd4c4`.
FontTools 4.61.1 selected the 2,136 Joyo kanji listed by the Agency for
Cultural Affairs, plus ASCII, Latin-1, Japanese punctuation, hiragana,
katakana, and full-width forms. It removed the GSUB, GPOS, and GDEF tables,
which the Text outline compiler does not execute. Glyph outlines and metrics
were not edited.

Official Joyo index:
https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/kokugo_nihongo/kokugo_shisaku/joyokanjihyo_sakuin/index.html

The Regular selected face is 609,668 bytes (535,787 bytes with gzip level 6),
and the Bold selected face is 622,984 bytes (546,535 bytes with gzip level 6). For
comparison on the same host, an IPA Gothic subset across broad Japanese CJK
ranges was 5,545,516 bytes (3,890,692 gzip), while the corresponding Noto CJK
subset was 13,458,308 bytes (11,354,699 gzip). This smaller face deliberately
does not claim arbitrary CJK, emoji, or all-Unicode coverage; absent glyphs
produce the public `glyph-missing` result.
